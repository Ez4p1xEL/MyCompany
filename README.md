## About MyCompany

## Description
MyCompany is a company and employment system for Minecraft servers running Paper.   
Players start a company, hire other players, create custom positions with real permissions, and run it together — company-owned chest shops, employee orders with rewards, claimed company land, a members-only company store, and an expertise tree.   
Every day the company pays salaries and taxes, so money keeps moving between employers, employees, and the businesses they run.   
The whole system is managed from a single GUI with `/myc`.  
  
MyCompany needs your ideas and feedback to grow and reach the goal of immersive economic gameplay
